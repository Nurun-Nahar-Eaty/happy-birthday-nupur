Written With Du'a & Gratitude

For You, Nupur

Assalamu Alaikum,
I still remember our first meet, how you introduced yourself saying, "Ami-o Nirjhona-er friend." You looked so friendly literally the kind of person you look at and immediately feel at home with.

I don't remember most of the early days of university, just that they were beautiful. Time takes so much away, but I remember every version of you from all these years. Those versions will stay alive with me forever, and I love every single one of them so dearly.

During one of the hardest times in my life, you were right there beside me. I've received so many blessings from Allah, alhamdulillah, and you are truly one of them. At first, I was hesitant about becoming roommates because I was scared it might ruin our friendship. I couldn't have been more wrong, now I don't even know how I would have survived without you. I always thought my college days were the best time of my life, yet because of you, hostel life became a beautiful memory too. I miss those days so much: going shopping, heading to the bazaar, sorting out groceries and bills, and hustling through the life together.

A lot of my friendships have faded because I'm really emotionally avoidant. Distance makes it hard for me to push past that, so connections tend to drift. But you always stay in touch. Even if it's just a quick "hi" in a message, you always show up, and I really appreciate that more than you know.

You're such a good person, Nupur. I know life feels pretty heavy right now, and I keep you in my prayers. Even as I write this, I'm praying to Allah to take away whatever is weighing you down and make things easier for you, to give you all the good things you ask for.

One thing I really want to remind you: I know life gets busy and hectic, but please try to pray on time. Don't delay it, it genuinely makes a difference. Also, try waking up at least 20 minutes early for Fajr and praying just two short rak'ahs. I'm not forcing you at all, just sharing gentle advice from a friend who wants the best for you.
May your upcoming years be filled with real happiness and contentment.

Happy birthday, Nupur, my dearest friend 🌻

🌻 Comfort & Du'a Jar

Whenever Life Feels Heavy, Tap a Petal

Five gentle reminders and prayers tucked away for your heart whenever you need reassurance.

🌻
Petal I • Promise of Relief

Surah Ash-Sharh (94:5–6)

فَإِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا ۝ إِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا

"Verily, with hardship comes ease. Verily, with hardship comes ease."

Allah didn't say ease only comes after hardship—He promised that ease is woven right alongside it, even before you can see it.

🌿
Petal II • Gentle Strength

Surah Al-Baqarah (2:286)

لَا يُكَلِّفُ اللَّهُ نَفْسًا إِلَّا وُسْعَهَا

"Allah does not burden a soul beyond that it can bear."

You don't have to carry everything perfectly all at once. Allah knows the exact capacity of your heart and will never leave you unsupported.

🌼
Petal III • Morning Light

Surah Ad-Duha (93:5)

وَلَسَوْفَ يُعْطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرْضَىٰ

"And your Lord is going to give you, and you will be satisfied."

Not just given enough to get by—given until your heart feels genuinely content, peaceful, and at rest.

🌙
Petal IV • Relief From Anxiety

Prophet Yunus's Du'a (21:87)

لَّا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ

"There is no deity except You; exalted are You. Indeed, I have been of the wrongdoers."

Whenever your chest feels tight or overwhelmed, whisper this du'a—no believer calls upon Allah with these words except that He answers and brings relief.

🤲
Petal V • A Birthday Prayer For You

A Personal Birthday Du'a

اللَّهُمَّ أَزِلْ هَمَّهَا وَارْزُقْهَا السَّكِينَةَ وَالْبَرَكَةَ فِي الدُّنْيَا وَالْآخِرَةِ

"May Allah replace every hidden worry in your heart with lasting peace, barakah in your time, and joy in both worlds. Ameen."

You are never walking alone, Nupur. You are constantly remembered in sincere du'as, today and always.
